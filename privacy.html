What the app does

QBO Quick Docs is a private desktop tool that connects to QuickBooks Online to create estimates and invoices for the owner's own company.

Information accessed

When you connect, the app can read customers and products/services and create estimates and invoices in the QuickBooks Online company you authorize.

Storage

Connection tokens are stored only in a file on the owner's own computer. The app does not send QuickBooks data to any server operated by the owner or any third party, and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or disclose your information to anyone. The app communicates only with Intuit's QuickBooks services.

Security and retention

The token file stays on the local computer and is deleted when the app is removed. Access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the app in QuickBooks Online.
